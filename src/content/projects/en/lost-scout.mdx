## 3D puzzle and exploration game

Lost Scout is an academic game with low-poly visuals and a free camera. The goal is to reach camp by solving puzzles in each level using objects in the environment, including logs, ropes, ladders, pressure plates, and levers.

### My role

I built the options menu and integrated it into the pause menu. I also implemented the credits window and worked on the island animation and the lighting for the puzzle lever.

### Technical approach

The team built the game in **Unity** with **C#** and used **3D Studio Max** for modeling. The puzzles combine exploration with interactions that open the way to camp.
